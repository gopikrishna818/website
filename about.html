Engineering Creed

My Core Operating Principles

↳ Measure in business outcomes, not F1 scores.

A 99% accuracy model that stays inside a Jupyter Notebook creates exactly $0 in business value. I optimize for production latency, cost, and active integration.

↳ If it can't run in production, it doesn't count.

Engineering means planning for edge cases, retry loops, API rate-limits, database scaling, and deployment stability—not just training models on clean datasets.

↳ Read the paper. Reproduce the result. Then improve it.

No black boxes. I audit model layouts, evaluate vector embedding pipelines at a systems level, and build custom router guards rather than relying on generic wrappers.

Intellectual Depth

5 Papers That Shaped My Engineering

1. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (Lewis et al.)

Takeaway: Proved parametric memory must be dynamically grounded with non-parametric retrieval to solve hallucination in production.

2. DocTR: Document Text Recognition (Mindee)

Takeaway: Demonstrated ensembling deep CNN layout classifiers with transformer sequence decoders yields peak OCR reliability.

3. Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani et al.)

Takeaway: Swapped sequential recurrence for parallelized self-attention, unlocking the transformer scaling era.

4. Lost in the Middle: Long Contexts (Liu et al.)

Takeaway: Proved LMs hallucinate when details sit in the middle of long prompts, rendering high-fidelity re-ranking indispensable.

5. PP-OCR: Practical Ultra-Lightweight OCR

Takeaway: Demonstrated that aggressive pruning, quantization, and custom downsampling can shrink model sizes by 4x without sacrificing production accuracy.
